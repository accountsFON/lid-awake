# Changelog

## 1.9.0 (2026-10-18)

First public release.

- Keeps a MacBook running with the lid closed, with no timer or a 1, 2, 4, or 8 hour timer.
- Locks the Mac and mutes sound the moment the lid closes, and restores sound when it opens.
- Turns the built-in screen's backlight off behind the closed lid and restores your brightness on open.
- Master Power Mode: a virtual monitor while the lid is closed so AI computer use keeps working, plus a lock the moment the lid opens.
- Automatic safety: normal sleep returns when the timer ends, when the battery drops to 15% unplugged, or when the app quits.
- An optional no-password rule so Turn On never asks for your password.
- A Help window that explains every option.
- Universal build for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 13 or newer.
